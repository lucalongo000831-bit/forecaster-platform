# Political disclosure limitations

- US congressional disclosures can appear weeks after a trade; delay is displayed and measured.
- Values are usually statutory ranges, not exact consideration. Midpoints are estimates, never reported facts.
- Disclosures are not complete real-time portfolios and may omit economic exposure that cannot be mapped reliably.
- Asset descriptions, options and private/non-market assets can be ambiguous. Unresolved rows remain visible and excluded from instrument analytics.
- Amendments and provider duplicates occur. Logical deduplication is versioned and the removed count is reported.
- Party, district, owner and source links can be absent in upstream data.
- FMP access, history and pagination depend on the configured subscription and rate limits.
- `PROVIDER_ONLY` does not mean official verification. Official comparison can produce `OFFICIAL_SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `SOURCE_MISMATCH`, `PENDING`, or `UNVERIFIABLE`.
- Cluster activity means multiple unique members disclosed the same side within a window. It does not establish coordination, intent, legality, privileged information or causation.
- Post-disclosure returns are historical observations, not investable recommendations.
